## A. THREE BIG HEADLINES (1–3)

### 1. IRAN SAYS TWO TANKERS HIT MINES IN STRAIT OF HORMUZ
- Region: Middle East — Source: Reuters / Al Jazeera — Pin: Strait of Hormuz (26.6N 56.3E)
- Iran's Revolutionary Guard Navy said two oil tankers struck naval mines and exploded and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday, calling the ships violators that had used routes it had not authorised. The mine claims were unconfirmed, and Iran said it was not worried about the US naval blockade.
- Illustration: a narrow strait between two cliffs with two burning tankers, black smoke columns, and a small mine bobbing in the water.

### 2. SHENZHOU-24 DOCKS WITH FIRST FOREIGN ASTRONAUT ABOARD
- Region: Asia — Source: Reuters / Xinhua — Pin: Jiuquan, China (41N 100E)
- China launched Shenzhou-24 from Jiuquan on Wednesday morning and docked with the Tiangong space station about three and a half hours later. Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, became the first foreign astronaut to board Tiangong and the first Pakistani in orbit, and will stay about a week.
- Illustration: a white spacecraft docking with a space station above Earth, with a Pakistani flag and a Chinese flag on its side.

### 3. COLOMBIA FREES ALL CARTAGENA HOSTAGES
- Region: South America — Source: BBC / AP — Pin: Cartagena, Colombia (10N 76W)
- Colombian security forces stormed an Exito supermarket in Cartagena early on Wednesday and freed about 20 remaining hostages after a 17-hour siege, killing one captor. President Abelardo de la Espriella said all hostages were rescued, after negotiators had earlier won the release of 10 captives.
- Illustration: a supermarket front with a police cordon, a helicopter overhead, and officers leading freed hostages out the door.

## B. TEN SMALL STORIES WITH ILLUSTRATION (4–13)

### 4. HOUTHIS SAY THEY HIT RAS TANURA REFINERY
- Region: Middle East — Source: Reuters — Pin: Ras Tanura, Saudi Arabia (26N 50E)
- Yemen's Houthis said on Wednesday they had attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil refinery, with spokesman Yahya Saree saying the attack caused fires. The Saudi-led coalition said air defences intercepted four drones over Ras Tanura but did not confirm the refinery had been struck.
- Illustration: an oil refinery at night with a fireball rising from its towers and a small drone above it.

### 5. UKRAINE SAYS DRONES SET TWO RUSSIAN REFINERIES ABLAZE
- Region: Europe — Source: Reuters / AP — No pin (Samara refineries — keep pin count at 13)
- Ukrainian drones reportedly hit the Syzran and Kuibyshev refineries in Russia's Samara region overnight, setting both ablaze. One person was killed, and Russia said it downed 145 Ukrainian drones.
- Illustration: two oil refineries burning under a night sky, with a small drone silhouette above the flames.

### 6. ISRAEL KILLS HAMAS SECURITY CHIEF IN GAZA
- Region: Middle East — Source: Reuters — Pin: Gaza City, Gaza (31N 34E)
- Israel's military on Wednesday killed Mohammad Abuelouf, a Hamas colonel who heads the militants' interior security service, along with his wife and two children, according to Gaza's Hamas-run interior ministry and medics. The IDF said it targeted several military operatives in Gaza.
- Illustration: a damaged apartment block in a refugee camp with smoke rising, and a rescue worker carrying a stretcher.

### 7. ASIAN TECH STOCKS TUMBLE ON AI DOUBTS
- Region: Asia — Source: Reuters / CNBC — No pin (market story — keep pin count at 13)
- Tech shares fell across Asia on Wednesday: South Korea's KOSPI plunged 6.16%, with Samsung down 9.9% and SK Hynix down 11.1%, while Japan's Nikkei fell 2.78% and SoftBank fell about 15%. Investors fretted again over the AI boom and the debt funding it, and reports of new US tariffs unsettled markets.
- Illustration: a falling stock chart with a green computer chip sliding down a steep red line, and worried traders watching screens.

### 8. SUDAN DRONE STRIKES KILL 19 CIVILIANS
- Region: Africa — Source: Reuters — Pin: Damazin, Sudan (12N 34E)
- Sudanese rights group Emergency Lawyers said on Wednesday that drone strikes by the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces killed 19 civilians and wounded 20 others in two Blue Nile cities. Nineteen people, including six women, died in the capital Damazin, where drones struck three residential neighbourhoods overnight.
- Illustration: a dusty street of damaged houses with a small drone overhead and a Red Crescent worker helping a survivor.

### 9. AUSTRALIA AND SOLOMON ISLANDS SIGN SECURITY TREATY
- Region: Asia — Source: NYT / BBC — Pin: Canberra, Australia (35S 149E)
- The Solomon Islands and Australia signed a treaty in Canberra on Wednesday naming Australia the Solomons' primary security partner and barring any foreign military or security base. The Solomons notified China last month that its 2022 security pact would not be renewed when it expires next year.
- Illustration: two leaders shaking hands over a treaty document, with a map of the Pacific and small island flags behind them.

### 10. TUESDAY: HUAWEI CONVICTED OF BANK FRAUD AND TRADE-SECRET THEFT
- Region: North America — Source: AP / BBC — Pin: New York, USA (41N 74W)
- A jury in Brooklyn on Tuesday convicted Huawei of bank fraud and conspiracy to steal trade secrets, guilty on 10 of 12 counts, but acquitted it of racketeering, the most serious charge. Huawei said it would appeal, and Beijing accused Washington of obstructing Chinese firms.
- Illustration: a judge's gavel over a smartphone with a cracked screen, and a red "GUILTY" stamp.

### 11. TUESDAY: SENEGAL REINSTATED AS AFCON CHAMPIONS
- Region: Africa — Source: Reuters / NPR — No pin (court ruling in Lausanne — keep pin count at 13)
- The Court of Arbitration for Sport on Tuesday reinstated Senegal as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners, ruling the referee's decision to restart the final after Senegal's walk-off could not be challenged. Senegal's 1-0 extra-time win over Morocco in January stands, and Morocco vowed to pursue all legal avenues.
- Illustration: a football trophy on a podium with a judge's gavel beside it, and green-yellow-red confetti falling.

### 12. INDONESIA MINE TOLL RISES AS RESCUERS DIG ON
- Region: Asia — Source: SCMP / Al Jazeera — Pin: Bogor, Indonesia (7S 107E)
- Rescuers in Bogor, West Java, recovered three more bodies on Wednesday, raising the toll to six, with around 20 miners still trapped in a flooded illegal gold mine. Heavy rain made a nearby river overflow and flood the mine more than a day earlier.
- Illustration: a flooded mine entrance with rescue workers in helmets pumping water and carrying a stretcher.

### 13. TRUMP SET TO IMPOSE NEW TARIFFS ON 16 ECONOMIES
- Region: North America — Source: AP / BBC — No pin (reported plan, Washington)
- The Trump administration is preparing new tariffs on 16 economies, including China, the EU and Japan, AP sources said on Wednesday, after a monthslong probe into industrial subsidies. The plan could be unveiled soon, though the timing could still change, after the Supreme Court struck down earlier tariffs.
- Illustration: a stack of shipping containers stamped with tariff labels, and a hand holding a "TARIFF" sign over a cargo ship.

- Region: Europe — Source: Reuters — Pin: Odesa, Ukraine (46N 31E)
- A Russian strike killed two people and injured three, including a child, in Odesa overnight, the regional governor said Wednesday, while an attack on Kyiv set fire to a multi-storey apartment building and injured two people.

### 15. LIBYA VOWS RETALIATION AFTER TWO OFFICERS DIE
- Region: Africa — Source: AP / Reuters — No pin (Benghazi — keep pin count at 13)
- The Libyan National Army on Wednesday vowed unspecified retaliation after confirming two naval officers died of wounds from Sunday's Ukrainian drone attack on the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi. Italy said Ukraine was wrong to attack in Libyan waters; France said Kyiv acted in self-defence.

### 16. MERZ WARNS RUSSIA WANTS MORE THAN UKRAINE
- Region: Europe — Source: Reuters — No pin (Bundestag speech, Berlin)
- German Chancellor Friedrich Merz told the Bundestag on Wednesday that Russia is escalating threats to European security and "wants more than Ukraine", as he defended higher defence spending and continued aid to Kyiv.

### 17. BBC: 490,334 RUSSIAN WAR DEAD
- Region: Europe — Source: BBC — No pin (data analysis)
- At least 490,334 Russian soldiers have died since the start of the war in Ukraine in 2022, according to leaked Russian government statistics analysed by the BBC, drawn from more than 11 million death records hacked on 1 November. The Kremlin declined to comment.

### 18. RUSSIAN DRONE ENTERS POLISH AIRSPACE
- Region: Europe — Source: Fox News / UPI — No pin (airspace incident — keep pin count at 13)
- A Russian drone entered Polish airspace overnight, days after three Russian missiles crossed into Poland on 20 November. Warsaw renewed its call for a permanent US troop presence, and Moscow rejected the airspace claims.

### 19. TUESDAY: ABREGO GARCIA LEAVES US FOR SPAIN
- Region: North America — Source: NYT / BBC — No pin (departure, destination Spain)
- Kilmar Abrego Garcia voluntarily left the US for Spain on Tuesday night, ending a nearly two-year legal fight after he was wrongfully deported to a Salvadoran prison in March 2025. He said "I am leaving happy", and Homeland Security said he had self-deported.

### 20. TUESDAY: SULLIVAN CONCEDES ALASKA RACE TO PELTOLA
- Region: North America — Source: NYT / AP — No pin (election result, Alaska)
- Senator Dan Sullivan conceded defeat to Mary Peltola on Tuesday, a week after AP called the Alaska race, which Peltola won by more than four percentage points. The Justice Department had abandoned a lawsuit over the race five days earlier.

### 21. UNCLASSIFIED CIA SYSTEM BREACHED
- Region: North America — Source: AP / France 24 — No pin (Langley — keep pin count at 13)
- Hackers breached an unclassified CIA analysis system, exposing some unclassified information, an AP source said Wednesday, and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence was also affected. The Treasury's FinCEN is probing a separate incident.

### 22. IOWA ARMY TO DETONATE FIVE TONS OF LEAD AZIDE
- Region: North America — Source: AP / UPI — Pin: Middletown, Iowa, USA (41N 91W)
- The US Army will detonate 68 drums of lead azide, about five tons, at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant at 9 a.m. on Wednesday, after the date changed several times over public concern. Officials say there is no reason to panic.

### 23. TUESDAY: DOW CLOSES ABOVE 50,000 FOR FIRST TIME
- Region: North America — Source: AP / Reuters — No pin (market close, New York)
- The Dow closed above 50,000 for the first time on Tuesday, up 0.9% to 50,018.70, while the Nasdaq fell 1.5% and the S&P 500 fell 0.5%, its eighth loss in nine days.

### 24. TUESDAY: CHINA PROBES POLITBURO MEMBER CHEN JINING
- Region: Asia — Source: Reuters — No pin (Shanghai — keep pin count at 13)
- Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, a member of China's elite Politburo, is under investigation, state-run Xinhua said on Tuesday. He is the fourth member of the top leadership body to be probed since 2025.

### 25. BANGLADESH GARMENT WORKERS WIN 36% PAY RISE
- Region: Asia — Source: Al Jazeera / The Hindu — No pin (Dhaka — keep pin count at 13)
- Bangladesh's wage board agreed to raise the garment minimum wage from 12,500 to 17,000 taka a month, a 36% rise, after weeks of protests. Unions had demanded at least 18,000 taka, and border guards were deployed across Dhaka.

### 26. HANOI RESTAURANT BLAST TOLL RISES TO SEVEN
- Region: Asia — Source: VnExpress / AP — No pin (Hanoi — keep pin count at 13)
- The toll from Tuesday's gas explosion at a hotpot restaurant in Hanoi's Dong Da district rose to seven dead and nine injured on Wednesday. Authorities suspected a cooking gas leak.

### 27. BUS CRASH KILLS 13 IN RAS AL KHAIMAH
- Region: Middle East — Source: Gulf News — Pin: Ras Al Khaimah, UAE (25.8N 55.9E)
- A bus hit a stationary truck in Ras Al Khaimah on Wednesday, killing 13 people and injuring 14, in one of the deadliest road crashes in the UAE in recent years.

### 28. IRAN FLOGS SINGER 74 TIMES OVER UNVEILED CONCERT
- Region: Middle East — Source: France 24 / Fox News — No pin (Qom — keep pin count at 13)
- Singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four team members were each given 74 lashes in Qom over an unveiled concert, reports said Wednesday. The UN called the punishment abhorrent and urged Iran to abolish it.

### 29. POPE LAMENTS FAILURE OF COP31 CLIMATE TALKS
- Region: Global — Source: AP — No pin (Vatican statement)
- Pope Leo XIV on Wednesday expressed concern over the failure of the UN COP31 climate talks to reach an agreement, saying extreme environmental events linked to the climate crisis are becoming increasingly frequent.

### 30. UN WARNS HIV GAINS COULD UNRAVEL
- Region: Global — Source: UN News — No pin (global report, Geneva)
- The UN warned on Wednesday that decades of progress against AIDS could reverse as international funding is cut. In Nigeria, 94% of people on treatment reached viral suppression, and a South African firm is preparing a monthly HIV prevention pill.

### 31. ZAMBIA INDICTS OPPOSITION LEADER FOR TREASON
- Region: Africa — Source: RFI / AllAfrica — No pin (Lusaka — keep pin count at 13)
- Prosecutors formally indicted Zambia's main opposition leader and 12 others on treason charges on Wednesday over an alleged plot to overthrow the government.

### 32. DR CONGO HALTS COBALT EXPORTS AGAIN
- Region: Africa — Source: Africanews / Le Monde — No pin (Kinshasa — keep pin count at 13)
- DR Congo will suspend cobalt exports from the end of the month to lift falling prices, its second such pause. Congo supplies most of the cobalt used in batteries.

### 33. ARGENTINA ENTERS TECHNICAL RECESSION
- Region: South America — Source: MercoPress / Buenos Aires Times — Pin: Buenos Aires, Argentina (35S 58W)
- Argentina entered a technical recession on Wednesday after two straight quarters of contraction. Activity rose 1.1% in September from August but fell 1.2% year on year, and bakeries reported collapsing sales.

### 34. TUESDAY: VENEZUELA REVEALS $237.5BN PUBLIC DEBT
- Region: South America — Source: Asharq Al-Awsat / Al Jazeera — No pin (national figures, Caracas)
- Venezuela said its total public debt is $237.5 billion in a macroeconomic framework published Tuesday, giving creditors a clearer view ahead of restructuring talks. Police separately clashed with protesters over the minimum wage near the presidential palace.

### 35. BEPICOLOMBO REACHES MERCURY ORBIT
- Region: Global — Source: ESA / CBC — No pin (deep space)
- The European-Japanese BepiColombo probe reached Mercury orbit on Wednesday after an eight-year journey since its 2018 launch. The space agency said it is on track and safe despite a glitch.

### 36. SOORYAVANSHI, 15, SMASHES RECORD 204 OFF 53 BALLS
- Region: Middle East — Source: BBC Sport — No pin (Doha — keep pin count at 13)
- Fifteen-year-old Indian batter Vaibhav Sooryavanshi scored 204 runs off 53 balls for India A against the UAE in the Asia Cup Rising Stars in Doha on Wednesday, the fastest double century in List A cricket.
